FAQ

Your questions, answered.

What does a "fractional" executive assistant mean?

You get experienced executive and project support for a defined share of the month, without hiring full-time. It's direct access to me, not a rotating team: the same person learns your priorities, your people, and how you like things done.

What's the difference between ongoing and project support?

Ongoing support is a monthly retainer for the daily load: calendar, inbox, travel, follow-ups. Project support is a scoped engagement for one thing with a clear finish line, like an event, venue search, or launch logistics. Many founders combine the two.

When are you available, and how fast do you respond?

Working hours and response expectations are agreed in your scope before we start, along with how urgent requests and extra work are handled. The promise is proactive ownership, with a rhythm you can rely on.

Do you work with founders outside Atlanta?

Yes. I'm based in Atlanta and work remotely with founders anywhere, across time zones, using the tools your team already runs on.

How do you handle confidential information?

Discretion is part of the job. I only use the access you grant, keep sensitive information out of shared or personal channels, and am happy to sign an NDA before we start.

What decisions do you make on my behalf?

We decide that together during scoping. Typically I handle scheduling, logistics, and vendor back-and-forth on my own, and bring you anything involving spend over an agreed amount, commitments in your name, or a judgment call only you should make, with a recommendation attached.
